Skyjo audio assets
==================

These small MP3 assets are Freesound preview files from Creative Commons 0 sounds.
They can be used without attribution, but the source trail is kept here so future
agents know why they are safe to ship.

- `card-flip.mp3`: "flipCard.wav" by Splashdust, Freesound sound 84322, CC0.
- `card-pickup.mp3`: "Shuffle cards" by Breviceps, Freesound sound 447918, CC0.
- `card-place.mp3`: "Pounding Cards On Table" by HogantheLogan, Freesound sound 466789, CC0.
- `table-ambience.mp3`: "Roomtone Bedroom Small Quiet" by leonelmail, Freesound sound 427838, CC0.
